Question 3 · Risk management

Three risks, three answers

Empty hubs

Hubs are a fixed cost. If customers don't sign up, we pay for idle machines.

Pilot 3 hubs before building 25. The public laundromat fills the gaps.

Customers trading down

Someone who would have paid full price now pays 9% less.

Test the €420 price on a small group first.

Reputation

We handle people's clothes and people's jobs.

Clothes insured from day one. No cleaner loses hours: the hubs create work.

Finance tracks each hub's break-even fill rate monthly. Flexi-jobs are largely excluded in the cleaning sector, so only for hubs and logistics, after a legal check.
